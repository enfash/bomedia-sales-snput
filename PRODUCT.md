# Product

## Register

product

## Users
BOMedia owners, cashiers and staff use this internal large-format printing sales app on desktops and staff phones.

## Product Purpose
Record jobs, money, expenses and stock, and review business activity. Preserve familiar staff screens while the staged PostgreSQL migration is reviewed in a separate sandbox.

## Brand Personality
Consistent, clear and familiar. The owner portal uses BOMedia blue; the cashier portal retains its orange identity. These roles and colors are defined in app/theme.ts.

## Anti-references
Avoid ad hoc component styles, heavy default Material shadows and all-caps buttons (CLAUDE.md). Avoid mobile controls that overflow the screen or squeeze action labels (owner review).

## Design Principles
- Reuse components and use app/theme.ts as the style source.
- Preserve existing business workflows and readable references.
- Keep selections and payment states explicit.
- Make staff-phone forms usable without losing entered work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keep text readable in both supported themes, provide visible control labels and focus states, and fit controls to narrow touch screens. No additional formal accessibility certification has been specified.
